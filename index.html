Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to hear back after I submit my application?

Our team typically reviews applications within 5–7 business days. If your profile matches an open role, you'll be contacted directly for the next steps. If you haven't heard back within two weeks, feel free to follow up with our recruitment team.

What does the interview process look like, and how many rounds should I expect?

The process usually involves 2–3 stages: an initial screening call with HR, a technical or role-specific interview with the hiring manager, and a final discussion (sometimes with senior leadership, depending on the role). Timelines vary but most candidates complete the full process within 2–3 weeks.

What documents do I need to provide once I'm selected, and how does onboarding work?

Once selected, you'll need to submit ID proof, educational certificates, previous employment/experience letters, and (if applicable) visa/work permit documents. Our HR team will share a checklist and guide you through offer signing, background verification, and onboarding formalities before your start date.
